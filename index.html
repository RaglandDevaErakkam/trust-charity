Who We Are

A Trust Built on Compassion. A Mission Driven by Change.

Puthu Ithayam Arakattallai is committed to supporting individuals and families facing financial and social challenges. Through meaningful initiatives in education, healthcare, livelihood development and hunger relief, we strive to create opportunities for people to improve their lives and build a sustainable future.

◎

Our Mission

To support underprivileged communities by providing essential resources, opportunities and assistance that improve their quality of life.

◉

Our Vision

A society where every individual has access to education, healthcare, nutritious food and opportunities to live independently with dignity.
